# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4, shadcn/ui (radix-nova, RSC, CSS variables, Lucide, base color neutral), motion. One site in this directory. Deploy target from the brief: GitHub repository `zweiradmeisterei` and Vercel production.

## Users

Inferred: people in Köln-Sülz and nearby who need a bicycle repaired, serviced, or advised, including owners of older bikes and e-bikes. They arrive with a concrete job (flat tyre, brakes, gears, inspection) and need a clear way to request a repair. Voice: formal German „Sie“.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for the Fahrradwerkstatt Zweiradmeisterei so a visitor can see what the workshop does, why it is a Meisterbetrieb, when it is open, and how to request a repair, call, or find Luxemburger Str. 265.

## Positioning

Meisterbetrieb in Köln-Sülz. The published claim on the existing site is that the owner works on the bike personally, and that every bike is treated with the same care. The site does not compete on price.

## Operating Context

Neighbourhood workshop on Luxemburger Straße in Sülz. Visitors call, email, or come by during published hours. Repair requests go through the real channels already published: phone and email. No online booking system and no price list were provided.

## Product Principles

- Say only what the workshop has already published.
- Make „Reparatur anfragen“ the obvious next step.
- Show the street, the hours, and the phone without a hunt.
- Keep the page one screen of intent: workshop, not a catalogue.
- Treat every bike as in scope; do not rank customers by bike price.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Formal German. Visible focus, real button and link names, reduced-motion support for any motion. The existing site also names work on Kinderwagen, Rollstuhl, and Rollator; that range stays visible rather than hidden behind a sport-bike story.
