# Home

Scope: one-page marketing site plus Impressum and Datenschutz stubs.
Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: people in Köln-Sülz who need a bicycle repaired. Job: understand the workshop and request a repair. Action: „Reparatur anfragen“ opens the published email; phone is the second action. Proof: published hours, address, Meister facts, and the service list from the existing site. Constraints: formal Sie; no prices, reviews, or weekend hours; generated images are stand-ins.

Memorable moment: the enamel board’s live row is a working request, and 265 stays fixed in the bar.

Unresolved: weekend hours unpublished; full legal copy missing; real photographs missing. One-prompt mode skipped the direction picker. This session is code-led because no comp was approved; that path is not stored.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is the stop case on Luxemburger Straße — the next thing that happens is the repair. It refuses the CM4all stack of a centered headline, icon cards, and a phone-photo gallery.

OWN-WORLD: Daylight stucco ground, a committed enamel-blue case, porcelain type, one signal-orange live cell. Schibsted Grotesk, flush left, tabular times. Hairline rules. No icon tiles. House number 265 locked at the bar’s right edge.

STORY: The visitor sees the bike, the offer, and a working request, believes a Meister takes any bike, then asks, calls, or reads the hours and the map.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sticky enamel bar with the wordmark, links, phone, and 265. Below it, one framed case fills the rest of the first screen: a workshop photograph on top, visible at once, and an enamel board beneath. The board leads with the H1 „Reparatur für jedes Rad.“, a one-line request field, the orange action „Reparatur anfragen“, „Anrufen“, and the line Mo–Fr 9:30–18:00 · Luxemburger Str. 265. No eyebrow. No fade-in.

FORM: Assigned candidate 5, the Köln tram-stop case, seed c554f9da. Yellow split-flap material is translated into the pinned clean neighbourhood workshop (enamel, daylight, one orange live row). Topology kept: one case, a live next row, a timetable, time as the axis. Signature interaction: a single sheen across the already-visible orange control, via motion/react, cancelled under reduced motion.

Raises, named: sewing-pattern commitment line (only the request row is orange); azulejo panel breaks (Meister and hours are seamed panels); datamatics restraint (type and rules, no icon tiles); teletext corner address (phone and street stay in the bar); kit crest lockup (265 fixed on every width).
